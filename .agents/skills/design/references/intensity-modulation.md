# Visual Intensity Modulation: Amplification vs. Restraint

Guidelines for adjusting visual intensity ("bolder" vs. "quieter") across slides and documents without losing design system integrity.

## 1. Making a Design Bolder (Amplification)

"Bolder" is an amplification request within an established design system, not an excuse to invent new fonts, colors, or random decorative noise:

- Amplify what the system already owns: Increase the scale of display typography, strengthen the primary accent, or enlarge the primary vector diagram.
- Commit to ONE decisive move: Make one dominant structural statement completely, then quiet everything around it. If every element gets louder, the entire composition becomes flat and noisy.
- Create dynamic rhythm: The bold element should feel like an intentional peak in the presentation sequence, contrasting with quieter adjacent slides.
- Do not add decorative clutter: Boldness comes from scale, conviction, and contrast, not from added borders, floating badges, or gradients.

## 2. Making a Design Quieter (Restraint and Subtlety)

"Quieter" means reducing visual aggression, sensory overstimulation, and noise while preserving personality and sharpness:

- Reduce color saturation: Shift bright chromatic accents down to 70% to 85% saturation.
- The 10% Accent Rule: Allow neutrals (slates, off-whites, dark grounds) to do 90% of the visual work; reserve chromatic color strictly for the 10% focal point.
- Tinted neutrals over flat gray: On colored backgrounds, derive secondary text and borders from the background hue or foreground color rather than washed-out generic gray.
- Soften typography weights: Replace heavy ultra-bold weights (900/800) with refined medium/semibold weights (600/500).
- Increase whitespace: Expand margins, increase padding between distinct groups, and let the content breathe.
- Remove decorative flourishes: Strip out background gradients, drop shadows, and ornamental lines that do not encode functional meaning.

## 3. The Balance Principle

- Quiet without intent collapses into generic boredom.
- Bold without restraint collapses into chaotic noise.
- Every strong visual accent requires surrounding calm to be perceived.
